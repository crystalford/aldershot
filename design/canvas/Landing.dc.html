Scene by scene

Work in short sittings, one scene at a time.

A long video essay gets made over a week, not in one go. Each scene has five steps, and Aldershot only shows you the next one. Each day it suggests a few scenes you can finish in about twenty minutes.

Every shot is a choice

Every shot starts as a sketch.

Click a shot to see what Aldershot recommends, and why. Keep the sketch, have Scout find real footage, use your own, or generate a picture based on the sketch. Generated pictures are never photorealistic, and when one appears next to a factual claim it’s labelled as a depiction.

You set one budget for the whole film — free, a little, or more — and every recommendation stays within it.

Scout

Scout finds what your footage is missing.

Scout looks for evidence for your claims, the other side of the argument, where a clip originally came from, and other people making the same point. It searches NASA’s library, public archives, podcasts, the news and your own recordings. It links to what it finds instead of downloading it, and checks whether you’re allowed to use it.

Pricing

You pay to process footage. Editing is free.

A video is processed once, when you first add it. After that, every edit, short and change is free. The only other cost is generated pictures — and only for the seconds that end up in your finished film, up to a monthly limit you choose.
