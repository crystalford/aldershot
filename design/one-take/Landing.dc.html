THE ARCHIVE DIRECTS THE NEXT ONE

Everything it reads, it keeps — as moments, sorted by what was said.

Footage is read once and broken into moments, each tagged by thread. Months later, ask for every time he contradicts himself and it hands back the moments across every video you've fed it — the reason each one matched, never a score. That's how a clip becomes a supercut, and a supercut becomes an essay. And it's why a sentence like keep every claim about the numbers can be a rule at all.

BEFORE YOU POST

It checks the things that get videos taken down.

Third-party clips, music underneath, faces, anything generated, missing attributions — each one named, each with a one-press fix and a recommendation. A provenance record goes on every export, listing every source and every edit.
