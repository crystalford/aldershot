## Wave 01 — Widow's Bay (Apple TV+) · **FINALE Wed Jun 17, ~8pm ET**

_Horror-comedy, created by Katie Dippold. Matthew Rhys as Tom Loftis, widower mayor
trying to turn a cursed New England island into the next Martha's Vineyard; Stephen
Root as the town doomsayer. "Stranger Things for grown-ups," retro 70s/80s horror._
**Window: finale week — peak engagement, then a sharp post-finale discourse spike.**

### ⭐ Angle A (recommended) — "The Mayor Was the Monster"
Video essay arguing the show's real horror is **gentrification**: Tom's dream of turning
a cursed town into a resort *is* the curse — commodifying a place destroys what it was.
The "malevolent force" is the market. Insightful, finale-timed, rewatch-bait. ~3–4 min.

### Angle B — "Why 2026's Scariest Show Is a Comedy"
Genre essay placing Widow's Bay in the retro-horror-revival lineage (Stranger Things,
King adaptations) and arguing comedy is now horror's best delivery system. Broader reach,
catches the finale + genre conversation. ~3 min.

### Angle C (meme/short) — "POV: your mayor when the whole town says it's cursed"
Short-form: Tom's cheerful denial vs mounting supernatural evidence as the universal
template for **any leader ignoring obvious red flags** (boss, politician, group chat).
Fast, remixable, high share velocity. <30s.
