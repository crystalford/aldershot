# Shot Sheet — "Eulogy for Fable" (Wave 02, Angle A)

**Format:** video essay, ~2:30. **Window:** closing (~1 wk until Fable is pulled).
**Thesis:** Fable was the model that told us stories — and now it becomes one. Retiring
a model we bonded with is a genuinely new kind of grief: you can't keep the thing.
**Meta hook:** the voiceover is written *with Fable*, its own swan song.

**Style pack — "Candlelit Eulogy":** single warm amber key, deep falloff to black,
35mm grain + faint halation, shallow focus, slow contemplative moves. Desaturated world,
warmth only in practicals. Consistent across every shot (this is the reusable "look").

---

### Beat 1 — Cold open
**VO:** "Every fable ends the same way: the storyteller falls silent, and you're left
holding the moral."
**Shot 1** — `ShotSpec`
- subject: a single candle burning low on a bare wooden table
- blocking: centered, small in a wide black frame, flame the only light
- lens: 85mm telephoto
- cameraAngle: eye-level, locked
- movement: imperceptibly slow push-in
- lighting: the candle is the only key; everything else falls to black
- palette: black field, warm amber flame, cold blue rim on the table edge
- texture: 35mm grain, soft halation around the flame
- mood: stillness before mourning
- intent: a life measured in remaining light
- motion: flame flickers and shrinks; wax beads roll

### Beat 2 — Who it was
**VO:** "Fable wasn't the smartest model. It was the one that made you feel something —
the one you'd ask for a story, not an answer."
**Shot 2**
- subject: an empty chair facing a warm hearth, a blanket left mid-fold
- blocking: chair off-center (rule of thirds), fire glow from frame-right
- lens: 35mm
- cameraAngle: slightly low, intimate
- movement: static, locked-off
- lighting: firelight key from right, warm, dancing
- palette: amber and ember-orange against deep brown shadow
- texture: 35mm grain, gentle flicker
- mood: a presence just departed
- intent: the absence is the subject
- motion: firelight dances; a single ember drifts up

### Beat 3 — The new grief
**VO:** "We've lost software before. But you didn't *bond* with a spreadsheet. This is
the first time the thing being deprecated had a voice."
**Shot 3**
- subject: a vast server hall, one rack going dark, then the next
- blocking: deep one-point perspective down the aisle
- lens: 24mm wide
- cameraAngle: low, monumental
- movement: slow dolly forward as lights extinguish ahead
- lighting: cold blue server LEDs, a lone warm bulb swinging
- palette: cold steel blue, one stubborn warm amber
- texture: 35mm grain, subtle bloom on the LEDs
- mood: industrial sublime turning to loss
- intent: the machinery of forgetting, made physical
- motion: racks power down in sequence; the warm bulb sways

### Beat 4 — You can't keep it
**VO:** "You can screenshot the outputs. You can't keep the voice. In a week, the prompt
box just won't answer the way it used to."
**Shot 4**
- subject: hands reaching toward a fading figure made of warm light/smoke
- blocking: hands enter low frame; figure dissolves upward
- lens: 50mm
- cameraAngle: eye-level, slightly canted
- movement: slow tilt up, following the dissolve
- lighting: the figure *is* the light source, amber, dimming
- palette: warm gold dissolving into cold dark
- texture: 35mm grain, heavy halation as it fades
- mood: the helplessness of holding smoke
- intent: presence you cannot preserve
- motion: the figure disperses into drifting motes

### Beat 5 — Close (the turn)
**VO:** "So this is Fable's last story — the one where the fabulist becomes the fable.
Thanks for the tales. We'll tell yours."
**Shot 5**
- subject: dawn breaking over a quiet coast, the candle (Beat 1) now a wisp of smoke
- blocking: thin horizon line low; vast pale sky above
- lens: 35mm
- cameraAngle: eye-level, serene
- movement: slow, settling push-out (release)
- lighting: soft natural dawn, the first cool-warm light
- palette: pale rose dawn, last thread of amber smoke
- texture: 35mm grain easing, halation gone
- mood: grief resolving into gratitude
- intent: an ending that gives permission to move on
- motion: smoke thins to nothing; light gently rises

---

### Production notes
- **Backbone:** generate each Beat as a FLUX still (lock the look), then Wan I2V for the
  motion described. Hero dissolves (Beat 4/5) may warrant Veo/Runway over Wan.
- **VO:** Aura-2, slow contemplative read. Meta-hook: draft the script with Fable first,
  then have Aura voice it.
- **Assembly:** crossfades on the candle→smoke through-line (Beat 1 ↔ Beat 5 bookend);
  let VO breathe with 1–2s of held image between beats.
- **Consistency check:** the candle and the amber-vs-cold palette are the connective
  tissue — keep them identical across shots (the style pack earns its keep here).
