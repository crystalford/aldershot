WHAT CHANGED IN THE CONVERSION
01It opens on the anecdote, not the thesis. The 37% lands as the hook; the thesis arrives at the end of act one, once you've felt the problem.
02Citations came out of the sentences and onto the screen. The voice paraphrases; the receipt card is verbatim, with its source. Nothing is read twice.

WHAT THIS BOARD DOESN'T SETTLE
The formula. These lines were rewritten by ear. The standard the voice column should follow — sentence length, where the breath goes, how a receipt is introduced — is the research still to come.
Record or read. Every SAID cell is a line to record, or to hear first in a scratch voice. {{ toCameraCount }} are you to camera, so they're recorded as video, not voice.
The scout. Nothing in the Archive is about insight, so {{ scoutCount }} beats here go out to be scouted — Archive first, then YouTube, candidates in one drawer marked Local or External, ranked by the transcript line that matched, never by a score. An essay from someone's own footage would mostly resolve in the first pass.
Exhibits. This essay has no PRIMARY EXHIBIT — no clip whose own sound takes the floor — because its evidence is text. A DSP or parliament essay would be mostly exhibits, with J-cuts leading into each one. The board can hold that; this essay doesn't test it.
