ACROSS ALL FOUR · what the product is missing
Speakers. Three of four sets have more than one voice. Moments need a speaker; rules need to name them (“everything Cllr Okafor said”); the cut needs on-screen names. Today there is no speaker anywhere.
Rules that protect, not cut. “Keep every claim and who made it”, “keep every definition in full”, “keep the clip with the reaction” — three new rule kinds (keep in full, pair, label) that the engine must honour before a cap or a drop.
The Read leads with the number that matters. “11 minutes of 184 are about the subject.” “Six to five.” “108 to 20.” It's a different first sentence for each kind, and only the vlog's is about themes.
Sources that aren't video. Slides with pages, a planning document with figures. Both are Documents; both want a page-to-time map; both make the Document block a product feature, not an essay feature.
Third-party footage up front. For react, Before-you-post isn't the last step — it's the Read. Which clips are someone else's, how long, attributed where.
Chapters. Long single-source footage wants chapters with titles more than shorts. Shorts mode's sibling.
